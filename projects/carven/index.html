Learning platform

CARVEN

Online courses combined with physical-center attendance, led alongside another developer.

App Store (opens in a new tab) Google Play (opens in a new tab)
Role
Led the project alongside another developer.
Platform
Flutter · iOS & Android

Problem

Learners needed online courses and in-person center attendance in one Arabic-first app, with a purchase flow that fits how its audience buys.

Solution

A Flutter app that unites recorded courses and exams with QR-based center attendance and a WhatsApp purchase flow, monitored through Firebase.

Engineering decisions

Structured for a small team

Dependency injection with get_it, declarative routing with go_router and BLoC/Cubit state keep features cleanly separated.

Arabic-first localisation

easy_localization with right-to-left support built in from the start.

Production observability

Firebase monitoring and analytics provide visibility into stability and usage after release.

Learning

  • Recorded video
  • PDFs
  • Exams

  • QR scanning
